What Mundus Actually Does

More than recruiting.
Your entire hiring function.

Most companies think of RPO as "outsourced recruiting." That description is technically accurate — and almost entirely misleading.

Mundus doesn't send you candidates. We run your hiring function. Every part of it.

From the first market mapping session to the signed offer letter — every stage is owned, managed, and accountable. Here is what that means in practice.

The Full Scope of a Mundus Engagement
01

Search & Sourcing

Before we post a single job or send a single message, we map the market. We identify where your candidates are, who they're currently working for, and what it would take to move them. Every outreach is written specifically for that person — not a template, not a blast.

Our average response rate is 34%. The market average is 11%.
02

Screening & Assessment

We don't filter CVs. We evaluate people. Every candidate who reaches your shortlist has been through a structured assessment covering technical fit, cultural alignment, compensation expectations, and our honest recommendation.

You receive three to five candidates per role — each with a written brief. Never a pile to sort through.

03

Interview Coordination

Scheduling, reminders, feedback collection, debrief facilitation — we manage the full coordination loop so nothing falls through and candidates don't go cold waiting for a reply.

Your time commitment: one 30-minute call per week. Everything else is managed by Mundus.

04

Offer Management

We handle the conversation that most founders dread. Offer construction, negotiation, competing offer management, and the close. We know what candidates actually care about — and how to present your opportunity in a way that lands.

All engagements include a 90-day replacement guarantee.
05

Market Intelligence

Every search generates data. Salary benchmarks, market availability, competitor movements, candidate feedback. We deliver this in weekly reports so your hiring decisions are informed by what's actually happening in the market — not what happened two years ago.

06

Employer Brand Representation

To every candidate we contact, we are your recruiting team. Every touchpoint reflects your culture, your values, and your employer brand.

Great candidates remember how they were treated before they joined.

07

Hiring Process Design

For companies without an existing HR function, we don't just fill roles — we build the infrastructure. Job leveling, interview frameworks, feedback structures, offer templates.

The process we build during the engagement is yours to keep.
